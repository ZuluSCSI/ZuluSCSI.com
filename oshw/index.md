# ZuluSCSI Open Source Hardware Models

Rabbit Hole Computing has released several ZuluSCSI designs under the [CERN OHL-S 2.0](https://spdx.org/licenses/CERN-OHL-S-2.0.html) (strongly reciprocal variant) Open Source Hardwware license.


* [ZuluSCSI Blaster Ultra Lite OSHW](https://github.com/ZuluSCSI/ZuluSCSI-Blaster-Ultra-Lite-OSHW) - Released in November 2026
* [ZuluSCSI Ultra Wide OSHW](https://github.com/ZuluSCSI/ZuluSCSI-Ultra-Wide-OSHW-RP2350B) - Released in March 2026
* [ZuluSCSI Pico OSHW](https://github.com/ZuluSCSI/ZuluSCSI-Pico-OSHW) for Raspberry Pi Pico 1/1W/2/2W - Released in October 2023
