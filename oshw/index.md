# ZuluSCSI Hardware

Rabbit Hole Computing has released several ZuluSCSI designs under the [CERN OHL-S 2.0](https://spdx.org/licenses/CERN-OHL-S-2.0.html) (strongly reciprocal variant) Open Source Hardware license.


* [ZuluSCSI Blaster Ultra Lite OSHW](https://github.com/ZuluSCSI/ZuluSCSI-Blaster-Ultra-Lite-OSHW) - Released November 2026 - [OSHWA certification](https://certification.oshwa.org/us002873.html)
* [ZuluSCSI Ultra Wide OSHW](https://github.com/ZuluSCSI/ZuluSCSI-Ultra-Wide-OSHW-RP2350B) - Released March 2026 - [OSHWA certification](https://certification.oshwa.org/us002798.html)
* [ZuluSCSI Pico OSHW](https://github.com/ZuluSCSI/ZuluSCSI-Pico-OSHW) - Released October 2023 - [OSHWA certification](https://certification.oshwa.org/us002581.html)
